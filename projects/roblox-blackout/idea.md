# Episode Concept: The 73-Hour Roblox Blackout

- **Working Title**: The Day One Burrito Broke Roblox (Or Did It?)
- **Format**: Explainer / High-Retention Faceless Documentary (16:9 1080p)
- **Target Audience**: Gaming enthusiasts, Roblox players, tech and distributed systems enthusiasts.
- **Angle**: Investigating the most legendary platform crash in gaming history. Unpacking why 50 million players blamed a Chipotle burrito promotion, contrasting it with the actual distributed systems engineering post-mortem from Roblox headquarters.
- **Why Now**: The October 2021 outage remains the gold-standard cautionary tale of microservices architecture and gossip protocol failure at massive scale.
- **Core Promise**: Cut through the internet memes to explain the real, high-stakes engineering breakdown that cost $25 million and locked out 43 million players for 3 full days.
